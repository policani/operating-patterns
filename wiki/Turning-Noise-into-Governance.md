# Turning Noise into Governance

## Situation pattern

The organization has plenty of information but not enough usable signal. Meetings, dashboards, intake channels, status updates, and informal escalations all exist, but they do not reliably help the right people make the right decisions.

## Useful move

Design governance around the decisions the organization actually needs to make. The goal is not more process. The goal is a clearer path from information to decision to action.

## Signals to look for

- Duplicate forums with unclear purpose
- Requests arriving through informal paths
- Decisions revisited repeatedly
- Priority language replacing tradeoff analysis
- High activity with low decision closure

## Practices

- Identify the decisions each forum owns
- Reduce duplicate intake paths
- Create decision-ready summaries
- Track closure, deferral, escalation, and rejection
- Remove reporting that does not support action

## Demonstrated capabilities

Governance design, intake discipline, decision architecture, portfolio operations, executive cadence.
