# Recovering Delivery Signal

## Situation pattern

Delivery reporting exists, but leaders still cannot tell whether the work is healthy. Status may be green while dependencies, decisions, defects, adoption gaps, or funding assumptions are creating risk.

## Useful move

Rebuild the signal model. The question is not whether there is enough reporting. The question is whether the reporting helps the organization make better decisions sooner.

## Signals to look for

- Status does not match lived delivery risk
- Risks are recorded but not acted on
- Dependencies are described but not owned
- Milestones move without business impact analysis
- Escalations arrive too late

## Practices

- Separate status, risk, issue, dependency, and decision
- Define what each signal is meant to trigger
- Connect delivery risk to business consequence
- Create a short list of unresolved decisions
- Keep the signal model simple enough to sustain

## Demonstrated capabilities

Delivery governance, signal integrity, risk translation, executive reporting, operating cadence.
