# Partner and Ecosystem Governance

## Operating problem

Partner and ecosystem programs can become difficult to govern when incentives, enablement, delivery expectations, customer commitments, and internal ownership are spread across multiple groups.

When decision rights and evidence standards are unclear, the organization may confuse partner activity with partner value.

## Pattern

Partner governance should clarify the operating model: who owns which decision, what evidence shows partner value, how incentives align with outcomes, and how risks or exceptions are escalated.

## Typical interventions

- Clarify partner roles and decision rights
- Separate enablement activity from performance evidence
- Define governance for exceptions, funds, incentives, or commitments
- Track dependencies across internal and external stakeholders
- Translate partner risk into customer, revenue, operational, or compliance impact

## What good looks like

- Clearer partner accountability
- Better evidence for investment and enablement choices
- Stronger cross-functional alignment
- Fewer ambiguous escalations
- More credible partner performance narratives

## Evidence pattern

This pattern reflects generalized work across partner ecosystems, program governance, channel operations, enablement, and cross-functional delivery. Specific partners, programs, financial figures, and employer details are intentionally omitted.

## Demonstrated capabilities

Partner ecosystem governance, stakeholder alignment, incentive governance, evidence-based decision support, operating model design.
