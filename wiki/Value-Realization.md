# Value Realization

## Operating problem

Organizations often approve work based on expected value but manage it based on activity, status, or milestone completion. That gap makes it hard to know whether the work is producing the business outcome that justified the investment.

## Pattern

Value realization requires a practical bridge between the business case and the operating cadence. The goal is to keep value visible after approval, through delivery, adoption, and measurement.

## Typical interventions

- Clarify the value hypothesis
- Identify who owns the outcome
- Separate delivery milestones from value indicators
- Track adoption and operational readiness
- Keep assumptions visible as conditions change
- Revisit expected value when scope, timing, or risk changes

## What good looks like

- Stronger connection between investment and outcome
- Better resource allocation conversations
- Earlier detection of value drift
- More credible benefit narratives
- Clearer accountability for adoption and realization

## Evidence pattern

This pattern reflects repeated work in portfolio governance, transformation, revenue systems, and operating model contexts. Specific metrics and organization details are intentionally generalized.

## Demonstrated capabilities

Value realization, investment governance, business case discipline, adoption readiness, executive reporting.
