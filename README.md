# Portfolio and AI Governance Source

This repository is the public source bundle behind Marco Policani's hosted
Portfolio and AI Governance library.

Read the polished public destination first:

- [Portfolio and AI Governance](https://policani.net/governance/)
- [AI Resource Allocation Governance](https://policani.net/governance/guides/ai-resource-allocation-governance.html)
- [Portfolio landing page](https://policani.net)

The markdown in [`wiki/`](wiki/) is maintained as source material and version
history. It is not the preferred reader path for hiring managers, PMO leaders,
AI operations teams, or portfolio reviewers.

## What's here

The source notes organize public-safe governance material around recurring
operating problems:

- Making scattered demand easier to sort, route, and govern
- Turning executive tradeoffs into something visible and discussable
- Keeping AI work tied to business value, proof, ownership, and human review
- Building readiness, value, and partner-governance routines that can survive
  real delivery pressure

## Guardrails

The public material should not include:

- Former employer names
- Client names
- Company logos
- Screenshots
- Internal terminology
- Financial figures
- Exact dates
- Named systems, roadmaps, or internal processes
- Details that make a prior organization identifiable

The private evidence library can keep specific facts, metrics, dates,
employers, and source notes. The public material should stay generalized.
